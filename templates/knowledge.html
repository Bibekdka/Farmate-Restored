Post-Harvest Processing Guide
1. The Curing Process

Boiling helps gelatinize starch and distribute curcumin uniformly across the rhizome.

  • Alkalinity: Add 0.05% – 1.0% sodium carbonate
  • Odor: Removes raw "green" organic smell
  • Risk: Over-cooking spoils the natural color
2. Drying Techniques

Mechanical ⚡ 12 Hours

Tip: Slicing into 2mm pieces speeds up drying by 300%.
